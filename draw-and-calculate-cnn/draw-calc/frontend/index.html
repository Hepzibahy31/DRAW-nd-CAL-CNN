      How the training data is varied

      • Each character has 1 to 3 ways of being written
      • Random tilt, slant, size and shape
      • Shaky hand wobble and random pen thickness
      • Blur and noise on some images
      • Random shifts of up to 2 pixels at every epoch
      • Real handwritten digits from 43 people mixed in
